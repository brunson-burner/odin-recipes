Mashed Potatoes

Description

A great bowl of mashed potatoes starts with the right potatoes and a few simple techniques. Russets make these mashed potatoes light and fluffy, while warm milk and melted butter help create a smooth, creamy texture without cooling them down. You can add garlic for extra savory flavor, or keep them classic. This easy stovetop side is just as at home with a weeknight dinner as it is on a Thanksgiving or holiday table.

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds russet potatoes, peeled and cut into 2-inch chunks
  • 3 cloves garlic, peeled, or to taste (Optional)
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • Salt and ground black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Gather all ingredients.
  2. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add potatoes and garlic, if using: reduce the heat to medium and simmer until potatoes are easily pierced with a fork, about 15 minutes.
  3. When the potatoes are almost finished, heat milk and butter in a small saucepan over low heat until butter is melted.
  4. Drain potatoes and garlic well; return to the hot pot for 1 to 2 minutes to remove excess moisture. Gradually add the warm milk mixture while mashing until smooth and creamy, using only as much as needed to reach your desired consistency.
  5. Season with salt and pepper. Serve topped with extra butter, if desired.

Cook's Note

Add up to 1 cup shredded Cheddar or Parmesan for cheesy mashed potatoes, or top with crispy fried onions or crumbled bacon for extra flavor. Try piping the mashed potatoes into a casserole dish and baking until lightly browned on top for a festive holiday presentation. You can make the mashed potatoes up to 2 days ahead and keep them covered in the refrigerator. Reheat until heated through, adding a little milk and butter as needed to bring back their creamy texture.
